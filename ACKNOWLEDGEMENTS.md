## Reuse policy

The licenses linked above are permissive; these acknowledgements use project names and links
for identification and do not claim endorsement or use their logos.

Before incorporating external source or assets, record the exact origin, revision, affected
Pulse files and modifications. Preserve the applicable license and notices in the distributed
release. For MIT copies or substantial portions, retain the copyright and permission notice.
For Apache-2.0 code, include the license, retain applicable notices, mark changed files and carry
forward applicable NOTICE contents when present. A thank-you paragraph does not replace these
redistribution conditions.

Do not incorporate material whose terms do not permit the intended use. Leaving out an
acknowledgement does not authorize reuse. Pulse's own code remains under [MIT](LICENSE);
any future incorporated third-party material retains its applicable terms.
